Why it matters now

Cybersecurity is part of the submission.

Section 524B

Since March 2023, US law requires “cyber devices” to include cybersecurity information in premarket submissions — including an SBOM and a vulnerability-management plan.

Refuse to accept

From October 1, 2023, FDA may refuse to accept a cyber-device submission that leaves this information out. Federal Register ↗

2026 guidance

FDA's premarket cybersecurity guidance was reissued on February 3, 2026, aligned with the new quality-management rule (QMSR). FDA ↗
